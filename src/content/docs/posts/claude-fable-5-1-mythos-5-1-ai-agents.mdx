# Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1: What They Mean for AI Agents

Anthropic has introduced **Claude Fable 5.1** and **Claude Mythos 5.1**, its latest models for coding, knowledge work, research, and long-running agentic tasks.

The release marks another step in the evolution from AI assistants to **AI agents** that can plan, use tools, execute multi-step tasks, and work with less human supervision.

For developers building coding agents and AI-native applications, Fable 5.1 is especially relevant because it is designed for complex, long-running workflows.

## What Is Claude Fable 5.1?

Claude Fable 5.1 is Anthropic's latest generally available model for advanced coding and knowledge work.

The model supports a **1 million token context window** and up to **128K output tokens**, making it suitable for large codebases, long-running tasks, and complex agent workflows.

Anthropic reports that Fable 5.1 achieves **52.6% on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1** and **55.8% on Terminal-Bench 4.0**, demonstrating improvements in agentic coding and scientific research tasks.

Fable 5.1 is designed for workflows such as:

- Complex software development
- Debugging and code review
- Working across large codebases
- Long-running coding tasks
- Research and knowledge work
- Tool-using AI agents
- Multi-step autonomous workflows

## Claude Mythos 5.1 for Advanced Research

Claude Mythos 5.1 is based on the same underlying model as Fable 5.1, but with different safeguards.

While Fable 5.1 is generally available, Mythos 5.1 is currently available through Anthropic's trusted access programs. It is designed for advanced work in areas such as **cybersecurity and life sciences**.

This approach highlights an important trend in AI development: different workloads require different combinations of model capabilities, access controls, and safety safeguards.

## Why Fable 5.1 Matters for AI Agents

The most important part of the Fable 5.1 release is not simply another benchmark improvement.

It is the continued shift from **AI assistants to AI agents**.

Traditional AI applications often follow a simple pattern:

> Prompt → Model → Response

Agentic applications are more complex:

> Goal → Reasoning → Tools → Execution → Feedback → Iteration → Result

An effective AI agent needs to understand a goal, break it into steps, interact with external tools, maintain context, recover from failures, and complete a task over an extended period.

Fable 5.1 is specifically designed around these long-running and agentic workflows.
